Who is RankForge for?+

RankForge is being designed for students preparing for EAMCET 2027 in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Can I prepare at my own pace?+

Yes. The product is designed around manageable sessions, topic-level practice, and a plan you can adjust as you go.

Does RankForge guarantee a rank or college?+

No. Results depend on many factors. RankForge is a preparation tool and does not guarantee exam marks, ranks, admissions, or outcomes.

Can I sign in with Google or my phone?+

The interface includes Google sign-in and phone OTP entry points. Real OAuth and OTP delivery require backend endpoints and provider configuration; these are not connected in this preview.

Is this the official EAMCET website?+

No. RankForge is an independent preparation product and is not an official exam authority website.
